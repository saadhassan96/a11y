NOLA A11y Lab

The NOLA Accessibility Lab at Tulane University studies and designs human–AI systems that make communication, information, and public services accessible.

Projects span sign language and captioning technologies, reading supports, accessible data visualizations, and civic AI chatbots. They center the experiences of people with disabilities and their communities.

Highlights

Projects

Communication Accessibility

  • Sign language
  • Captions

AI-powered tools that help people learn, look up, and understand American Sign Language, and richer captions that convey emotion and non-speech information for d/Deaf and hard-of-hearing viewers.

See 14 publications Funded by LA Board of Regents

Information Access

  • Reading support
  • Data visualization

Reading supports such as customizable text simplification, and accessible data visualizations, including personal health information, that help people understand and act on what they read.

See 4 publications Funded by Lavin-Bernick Grant

Civic AI

  • Access
  • Broader context

Participatory approaches to AI that make public services accessible and connect residents, communities, and local governments, along with the broader social context of AI in public life.

See 2 publications Funded by NSF
